## What this means

- The main finding for September is the gap itself. Posting went from about one post a week over winter to none for six weeks.
- The account has 41 followers, so every post reaches a small audience. With numbers this small, one extra like can change a ranking, and small differences between posts should not be treated as meaningful.
- Likes and comments alone are a weak measure at this size. Saves and shares would say more about whether a post was useful to people, but they are not available through the current connection.

## Recommendations for October and November

1. **Keep to the queued schedule.** The queue in this repository has approved posts about twice a week from 14 October through to 20 December. Staying on that schedule is the single most useful thing, because September shows what a gap looks like: no activity to learn from at all.
2. **Do not change the mix of formats yet.** October will be the first month with Reels, carousels and singles side by side. Let at least one full month run as planned before deciding to do more or less of any format. The earlier data does not favour carousels or singles either way.
3. **Sort out access to reach, saves and shares.** Either update the Instagram connection in Zapier so it includes the insights permission (if Zapier offers it), or, if that is not possible, note these three numbers by hand from the Insights screen in the Instagram app once a month for each post. With 41 followers, these are the numbers most likely to show a real difference between posts.
