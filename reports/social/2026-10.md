Instagram (@equal_psychology), the Equal Psychology Facebook Page and the LinkedIn company page.
Prepared: Sunday 1 November 2026 (Melbourne time). Period covered: 1 to 31 October 2026.
Read-only: nothing was published, edited or deleted on any platform.

## In short

- October was the first full month back after the September gap: 11 Instagram posts (4 carousels, 3 singles, 4 Reels), 8 Facebook mirrors and 6 LinkedIn posts.
- Followers moved by one on each platform we could check: Instagram 41 to 42, LinkedIn 21 to 22 (since 20 October). Facebook was 3 at last check.
- Every Instagram post landed between 2 and 4 likes plus comments. The best were the Stoicism carousel, the Stoicism single and the "ADHD: the full picture, missed" Reel, all on 4. The gap between best and worst is two interactions, which is too small to read much into.
- Facebook mirrors got no reactions, comments or shares. LinkedIn posts got 0 or 1 like each.
- One month is far too few posts to settle anything about formats, topics or goals.
