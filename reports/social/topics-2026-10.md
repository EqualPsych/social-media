# Topics people are asking about: October 2026

Prepared 1 November 2026 from a web search scan of public discussion (forums, community boards and public news coverage) in Equal Psychology's areas. Every item is paraphrased in our own words. No posts are quoted, no usernames are kept and no individual person's post is linked.

These are never evidence. Each one is a topic idea only and must go through ep-research-skill before any claim is made.

Limit of this scan: web search returned few results it could confirm were from the last 30 days, so these are recurring themes rather than a strict 30-day count. The search tool is US-based, so Australian forum coverage was thin.

1. **Will my GP soon be able to diagnose and treat adult ADHD in Victoria, and what does that change for me?** People are confused by state-by-state GP reforms and which parts apply where they live. *Topic idea only: needs ep-research-skill before any use.*
2. **Why does an adult ADHD assessment take so long and cost so much, and what is actually involved?** Wait times and out-of-pocket costs come up again and again, along with what the steps are. *Topic idea only: needs ep-research-skill before any use.*
3. **How do I know whether it is ADHD or something else, like anxiety or burnout?** Adults ask how overlapping experiences are told apart. *Topic idea only: needs ep-research-skill before any use.*
4. **How do I get through the first Christmas after someone has died?** Practical planning questions: whether to keep traditions, skip them or change them, and how to handle the build-up. *Topic idea only: needs ep-research-skill before any use.*
5. **Is it normal that the lead-up to a holiday feels harder than the day itself?** A recurring theme in grief communities. *Topic idea only: needs ep-research-skill before any use.*
6. **Why do I feel low and lonely since retiring, when I expected to feel free?** Loss of routine, role and daily contact after work ends. *Topic idea only: needs ep-research-skill before any use.*
7. **How much should I worry about my parents as they get older, and how do I stop it taking over?** Adult children balancing care, worry and their own lives. *Topic idea only: needs ep-research-skill before any use.*
8. **How many psychology sessions will I need, and how do Medicare sessions and gap fees work?** Cost and session limits are a common barrier people describe. *Topic idea only: needs ep-research-skill before any use.* (Any fee or rebate content must state full terms and be confirmed with Matt.)
